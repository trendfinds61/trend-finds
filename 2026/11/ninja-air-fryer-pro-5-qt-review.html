Crispy Food With Almost No Oil

The whole point of an air fryer is hot, fast-circulating air that crisps the outside of food while keeping the inside juicy — with up to 75% less fat than deep frying. A light spray of oil is all you need, and for many foods you need none at all.

That makes the Ninja Air Fryer Pro a good fit for anyone trying to eat a little healthier without giving up fried food. Chicken tenders, fries, mozzarella sticks, and roasted vegetables all come out golden and crunchy, with far less oil than a pan or a deep fryer.
