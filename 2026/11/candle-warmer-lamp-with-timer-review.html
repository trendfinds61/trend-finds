Why flameless is better than burning

Burning a candle eats the wick and the wax from the top, and the flame can soot up the glass and the wall behind it. A warmer melts the wax evenly across the surface, so the candle burns — or rather, melts — down evenly and lasts noticeably longer. There is no smoke, no open flame, and no hot wax to spill, which makes it a safer pick for homes with children or pets, and for bedrooms and offices where an open flame is not welcome.
