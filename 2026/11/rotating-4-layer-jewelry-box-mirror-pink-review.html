Why Four Layers Beat One Big Box

One big jewelry box means everything ends up in a pile. Four separate layers mean you can sort by type: rings on one layer, studs on another, necklaces and bracelets below. Chains stay untangled, earring pairs stay together, and you can actually see what you own — which means you wear more of it.

The 360° rotation is the clever part. Instead of lifting trays out and putting them back, you just turn the layer you want toward you. Getting ready in the morning gets faster, and your jewelry stays protected from dust, scratches, and tangles in the meantime.
