What Makes the Sky High Mascara Different?

How to Apply Sky High Mascara for Maximum Length

Start at the base of your lashes and wiggle the brush upward in a zigzag motion, then pull straight through to the tips. That wiggle is what separates the lashes and coats them evenly. One coat gives a natural, everyday length; two coats give a full, dramatic look.

Do not pump the wand in and out of the tube — that pushes air in and dries the formula out faster. And replace your mascara every three months, since old mascara is a common cause of eye irritation.
