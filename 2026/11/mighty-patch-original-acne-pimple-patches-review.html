How Do Hydrocolloid Pimple Patches Work?

The patch also does a second job: it stops you from touching or picking the pimple. Picking spreads bacteria and makes spots last longer and scar more easily. A patch is a simple barrier that keeps your fingers off and keeps the area clean while it heals.

How to Use Mighty Patch Original

Using Mighty Patch is simple. Wash and dry your face first — the patch sticks best to clean, dry skin. Peel a patch off the sheet, place it right over the pimple, and press gently for a few seconds. Leave it on for at least 6 hours, or overnight, then peel it off and wash the area.

One tip: use the patch on a whitehead that has come to a head. It works best on pimples with a visible white tip. It will not do much for deep, under-the-skin pimples that have no opening yet — those need a different treatment.
