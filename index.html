🛒 Before you buy — what to check

1

Price vs value

Compare similar products first. A big “discount” means little if the starting price was inflated — check the price against 2–3 similar items.

2

Ratings & review count

A 4.5★ rating from 10,000 reviews is far more reliable than 5★ from 12 reviews. Read the 1★ and 2★ reviews too — they show the real problems.

3

Return policy

Make sure you can return it easily if it does not match the photos or stops working. Free returns beat a slightly lower price.

4

Warranty & support

For electronics and appliances, check the warranty length and whether the brand has real support in your country.

5

Seller check

On marketplaces, buy from the brand store or a seller with a long history and high positive feedback — not the cheapest unknown listing.

6

Shipping & delivery

Check delivery time and shipping cost before you pay. A “cheap” item with slow, expensive shipping is not a deal.
