About

Trend Finds is an independent product-review site run by Mumin Ahmed. We share honest reviews and hand-picked shopping finds across home, beauty, fashion, tech and more.

Each review looks at a product's features, price and buyer ratings as listed at the time of writing, so you can see what a product offers — and what it costs — before you decide. Prices and availability change, so always check the live price on the product page before ordering.

We do not make fake claims, and we never promise rankings or results. If a review does not state a price, a rating or a drawback, that is because the information was not in the review — not because it was hidden.
